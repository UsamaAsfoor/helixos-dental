Patient Intake Automation

New-patient forms straight into the chart, flagged and ready

New patient paperwork — medical history, dental history, HIPAA forms, insurance cards — is read by AI and pushed straight into Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental. Front desk reviews exceptions instead of typing for 15 minutes per patient.

Form submitted→AI reads + validates→Pushed to PMS
🛡️

Dental Insurance Verification

Coverage, deductible and frequency limits 24 hours before the visit

For every appointment on tomorrow's schedule, AI pulls eligibility, remaining benefits, deductible, and frequency limits (cleanings, x-rays, perio) from the clearinghouse and posts a one-page verification sheet to the chart. Front desk stops spending mornings on hold.

Schedule pulled→AI pulls eligibility→Verification sheet filed
📊

Appointment No-Show Predictor

Catches the no-shows before they happen

AI scores every upcoming appointment for no-show risk based on patient history, demographics, day of week, and time of day. High-risk patients get a personal text or call; low-risk patients get a normal reminder. Open chair time drops, production goes up.

Daily scan→Risk scoring→Personal outreach
🦷

Recall & Hygiene Reminder Engine

Brings due-for-cleaning patients back on the schedule

AI watches the recall list every day and reaches out to patients due for hygiene with a personal-feeling text, email, or postcard. Lapsed patients (12+ months) get a re-engagement sequence. Hygiene chair fills itself instead of staff cold-calling lists.

Recall scan→Tiered outreach→Self-schedule link
🔒

HIPAA Compliance Scanner

Catches PHI before referral letters and records leave the office

Before any record leaves the practice — referral letters, x-ray packets, billing records — AI scans for PHI exposure (wrong patient name, second SSN, the previous patient still in the email body). Flags every issue with a redaction suggestion and keeps an audit log for compliance.

Doc queued→AI scans for PHI→Redact + audit-log

Questions

Everything you want to know
before you book.

What does "connecting my data" actually mean?+
We access your existing business data — your sales platform, CRM, spreadsheets, ad accounts, booking systems, whatever you use — and configure your AI system to pull from those sources. No confidential or sensitive personal data is accessed without explicit permission.
I'm not technical at all. Will I be able to use what you build?+
Yes. That's the entire point. You don't configure anything. You don't learn a tool. You receive a daily report — in your inbox, on a dashboard, however you want it — and you read it like you'd read any other morning briefing. We handle everything behind it.
How does the 3-day foundation actually work?+
After the 15-minute discovery call and signed agreement, we schedule a remote working session with you. Day 1 we connect to your systems, Day 2 we install the foundation and seed your agents, Day 3 is the handoff — we walk you through everything live, confirm it's running, and the first daily brief lands. The five agents we ship at install are your starting line — more layer in monthly through the retainer.
Do I have to take the retainer?+
No. The foundation is a standalone deliverable. You get a fully functional system with no ongoing obligation. The retainer is where the crawl-walk-run happens — new agents, custom modules, strategy as your priorities shift. There's no pressure and no contract trap.
What if the 15-minute call reveals this isn't the right fit?+
Then we'll tell you. The call is free and there's no hard sell. If your business isn't a good fit for what we build, we'd rather tell you upfront than take your money and underdeliver. Either way, you leave with clarity.
How is this different from hiring a developer or a VA?+
A developer builds you something static and leaves. A VA does manual work every week that should be running on agents. We install agents that watch, reason, and act continuously — and we stand up the foundation in 3 days, not 3 months. The retainer is where we keep building.
